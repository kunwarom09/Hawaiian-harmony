General Shipping Information

This Shipping Policy provides general information concerning Hawaiian Harmony orders placed through our website and authorized checkout partners.

Fulfillment Commitment: We strive to fulfill and dispatch all confirmed orders expeditiously to ensure your bottles reach your doorstep safely and securely.
1

Order Processing

Orders are generally processed after payment has been successfully confirmed.

The product information supplied for this website indicates that orders are typically processed within approximately 1–2 U.S. business days (Monday through Friday, excluding statutory holidays).

Processing Schedule: Orders placed over the weekend or on public holidays are prepared for dispatch on the following regular business day.
