What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your browser or device when you visit a website.

They can help websites perform various functions, including:

Remember Preferences
Retaining user settings, language, and display choices
Analyze Visitor Behavior
Understanding navigation flows and user engagement patterns
Maintain Functionality
Ensuring smooth operation, secure browsing, and error detection
Measure Performance
Evaluating loading speeds, system responsiveness, and stability
Understand Marketing Activity
Evaluating promotional effectiveness and marketing outreach

Types of Cookies We May Use

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may deploy the following categories of cookies:

Essential Cookies

These support basic website operation and security. Without these cookies, essential functions such as page navigation, secure areas, and session authentication cannot operate properly.

Analytics Cookies

These help us understand how visitors interact with pages, such as:

  • Pages visited: Tracking which educational and informational sections visitors explore.
  • Time on page: Gauging how long visitors spend reviewing formula information and reviews.
  • Traffic sources: Identifying referring websites, search engines, and referral pathways.
  • Device types: Identifying mobile, tablet, or desktop viewports to optimize responsive design.
  • General engagement: Monitoring scroll depth, button interactions, and navigation efficiency.
Functional Cookies

These may help remember choices or improve website functionality, such as keeping track of volume selections or accessibility preferences to provide a smoother, more personalized experience.

Marketing Cookies

If advertising or affiliate technologies are used, cookies may help attribute traffic, measure conversions, or understand marketing performance across campaigns and partner networks.

Third-Party Technologies

Third-party services such as analytics providers, checkout providers, or advertising systems may set their own cookies on your browser or device.

Third-Party Policy Governance: Those services operate under their own privacy policies. We do not directly control third-party cookie management practices once you interact with external checkout or analytics providers.

We encourage you to review the individual privacy and cookie notices of any third-party payment platforms or services you engage with through our website.

4

Managing Cookies

You have full control over cookie permissions on your personal devices:

Browser Controls: You may block, remove, or restrict cookies using your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to decline all cookies or notify you whenever a cookie is offered.

Common Browser Settings References:

Google Chrome
Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies
Apple Safari
Preferences > Privacy > Block all cookies
Mozilla Firefox
Settings > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection
Microsoft Edge
Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies
Important Note on Functionality: Certain functions may not work correctly when cookies are disabled. Blocking essential cookies may impact checkout workflows, cart persistence, or site navigation.
