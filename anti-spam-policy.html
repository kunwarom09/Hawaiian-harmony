Our Commitment to Responsible Email Communication

Hawaiian Harmony supports responsible email communication.

Zero-Tolerance for Spam: We do not knowingly encourage unsolicited bulk commercial email (UCE/spam). Every commercial message sent under our control is guided by transparency, respect for subscriber preferences, and full compliance with applicable communication regulations.

Marketing emails sent directly through systems under our control should provide a reasonable method to unsubscribe at any time.

1

Email Standards & Marketing Controls

To uphold a trusted communication standard, Hawaiian Harmony implements the following protocols across marketing communications:

Accurate Header Data
Clear and truthful sender information identifying Hawaiian Harmony
Transparent Subject Lines
Honest headlines accurately reflecting message contents and offers
Visible Unsubscribe Options
Easy-to-find opt-out mechanisms located in every promotional broadcast
Prompt Processing
Timely honoring of all recipient unsubscribe requests and preferences
2

How to Unsubscribe

Where applicable, you may unsubscribe from marketing communications by using either of the following methods:

1. Click the Unsubscribe Link
Simply click the "Unsubscribe" or "Manage Preferences" link located in the footer of any promotional email you receive.
2. Contact Support Directly
Contact customer support and request removal from our promotional mailing list by email.

Transactional Communications Notice

Please note that unsubscribing from marketing or promotional emails does not disable essential transactional correspondence.

Transactional communications such as:

  • Order confirmations: Receipts, order details, billing records, and purchase verification.
  • Shipping notices: Carrier dispatch alerts, tracking links, and delivery status updates.
  • Refund updates: RMA approvals, return package receipts, and refund transaction status.
  • Customer-support responses: Direct answers to customer inquiries, inquiries submitted via our contact desk, or resolution messages.
Service Integrity Requirement: These transactional messages may still be sent when necessary to service an existing transaction, safeguard customer records, or fulfill our ongoing customer care commitments.
